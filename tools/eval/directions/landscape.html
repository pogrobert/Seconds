Hollis & Reed

Garden builders in the Cotswolds

Stow-on-the-Wold, GloucestershireSince 2004

We build gardens that look as if they were always there.

Stone walls laid the old way, paths that weather in, planting that fills out by the second summer. We are a team of eleven: wallers, carpenters and gardeners who build every garden we design, from the first trench to the last bulb.

What we build

  • Whole gardens

    Design and build together, for country houses and town gardens alike. One team from survey to planting.

  • Dry-stone walls

    New walls and repairs in Cotswold limestone, laid without mortar by our own wallers.

  • Terraces and paths

    Reclaimed flagstone, gravel and brick, laid to drain and to last.

  • Planting

    Borders, meadows and hedging, chosen for your soil and planted at the right time of year.

Every garden starts with a walk round it.

We come to you, walk the ground and talk through what you want. The first visit is free and there is no obligation.
